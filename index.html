Good to Know

Answers before you ask.

Do you really deliver across all of Los Cabos, not just Cabo San Lucas?+

Yes — that's the entire point of Pharmacy Los Cabos. Our drivers run fixed routes covering Cabo San Lucas, the Corridor, and San José del Cabo daily, not just the marina area.

Do I need a Mexican prescription for medication?+

Some medications require a valid prescription under Mexican pharmacy regulation. Send a photo of yours on WhatsApp and our licensed pharmacists will confirm what's needed before delivery.

How do I pay?+

Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay, or cash on delivery in USD or MXN. Your driver carries a card terminal if you'd rather pay at handoff.

Can you deliver to yachts and marinas?+

Yes — share your slip number and captain's contact and we'll coordinate delivery to the Cabo San Lucas Marina or Puerto Los Cabos.

Is there a minimum order?+

No minimum on essentials. A delivery fee applies based on your zone and time of day, always shown before you confirm.
